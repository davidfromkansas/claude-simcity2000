Post-mortems

Claudeville 3 · Attempt 1

Missed: 9,190 people when stopped in 2022. Homes were too far from jobs, so commutes failed.

Claudeville 3 · Attempt 2

Missed: peaked at 35,690 people in 2011, then traffic, power wiring and debt; stopped in 2017.

Claudeville 3 · Attempt 3

Missed: destroyed by an earthquake and fire in 2011 at 18,410 people.

Claudeville 3 · Attempt 4

Missed: stalled at about 30,000 people in 2021; low land value and ten loans.

Claudeville 3 · Attempt 5

Missed: stuck near 6,700 people in 2012; the road link to the neighbors was missing for 10 years.

Claudeville 3 · Attempt 6

Missed: stuck near 11,500 people in 2013. Every check passed, but three loans cost more than the city earned in tax.

Claudeville 3 · Attempt 7

Missed: no loans and no debt, but only 3,850 people by 2010. Growing from savings alone was far too slow.

Claudeville 3 · Attempt 8

Missed: stalled near 8,000 people in 2014. Every check passed, but dense-only zoning grew slowly and the second loan did not pay back.

Claudeville 3 · Attempt 9

Missed: stuck near 7,500 people by 2022. Demand was at maximum for years but there was no money for more land; a power plant burned down with no fire station.

# Claudeville 4 · Attempt 6 strategy (from claudeville-4/reflection.md)

Goal: 200,000 by Jan 2050, same map, Medium, disasters on.

## What five attempts show
- **Every city ended broke.** Measured in attempt 5: each $10k loan costs ~$513 a year for ever, and a person pays
  ~$0.11 a year at 8-9% tax. **A loan must bring ~4,800 people just to pay its own interest.** Attempts 4-5 got
  ~2,700-2,800 people per loan.
- **Fire is beaten while cash is positive** (attempt 5: no great fire for 46 years without tree parks). Negative cash
  blocks bulldozing, and then one fire takes the city (2048).
- **Taxes at 8% with weak demand stalled attempt 5 for five years**; 6-7% fixed it in a year.

## Plan: every loan must pay for itself
1. **Loans only for districts that fill** (homes and jobs together, ~5,000 people each), and only while the demand for
   their zones is high. Power plants, pumps, fire stations and links are bought from cash, when they are needed, not ahead.
2. **The loan guard is not overridden**, except to fight a fire.
3. **Taxes 7% from the start**, the controller between 6 and 10 (raise only while demand ≥ 1,800).
4. **Keep cash positive at all times** so fires can be fought; from 20,000 people keep ~$3-5k.
5. **Fire rules from attempt 5:** no tree parks, power plants spread out each with a station, airport far from homes.
6. **Airport** in the north-east corner but one row in from the map edge (x116-121 y1-4), line through it, own station.
7. **Never override an alarm for more than 2 steps.**
8. Same opening as attempts 4-5 (day 1, 2003 district, 2002 coal + pumps with loan #3, north district with loan #4, zoo).

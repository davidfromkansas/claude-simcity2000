# Attempt 5 strategy (from the attempt 1–4 post-mortems)

Goal: 100,000 people by Jan 2050. Same flat map, Medium, disasters on, loans allowed.

## The new idea: a high-value city
Tax income comes from land value. Attempts 2 and 4 stalled around 30,000 people with land value
~25 and loan payments above tax income. This time every choice protects land value:

- **Pattern of bands:** HOMES, SHOPS, HOMES, SHOPS, FACTORIES, SHOPS, HOMES ... Homes always touch
  shops (jobs, no smoke); factories always have shops on both sides as a buffer; factory bands are
  fewer and grouped toward the south.
- **Parks:** one column of small parks ($20 each) in every home segment, next to the power column.
- **Dense zoning** for homes and shops from the start in the north (near lakes and river).
- **Power plants in one place**, the river strip west of the avenue, far from homes; replace coal
  with cleaner plants later when money allows.
- **Seaport as soon as factory demand stalls**; stadium when home demand stalls.

## Keep (proven)
- Un-zoned power columns next to every cross street, linked at both ends; wired pump fields with a
  pipe main; check the save for unpowered/dry buildings every few game months.
- Fire station per block, police per two blocks, volunteer fire department, gambling/parking fines.
- Separate tax rates: homes 6–9%, shops 5–7%, factories 3–5%.

## Money
- At most 5 loans. Each new block must be paid mostly from savings.
- Cash reserve of $5,000 after year 5.

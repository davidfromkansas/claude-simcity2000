## Biggest levers (money per person, jobs per person)
1. **Land value -> big buildings.** Dense homes upgrade 1x1 -> 2x2 at land value 96 and 3x3 at 192 (our cities: ~20).
   A district built for land value (by water, parks/trees inside every block, police, no factories or plants
   nearby, schools) could hold several times the people per tile. *untried - researching*
2. **Dense shops and factories.** More jobs per tile of road/pipe/power; we only zoned light jobs. *untried*
3. **Microwave power from 2020** (1,600 MW for $28,000 vs coal 200 MW for $4,000): cheaper per MW, no smog
   next to homes. Requires playing past 2020 - we never have. *untried*
4. **Play to 2050.** Every attempt so far stopped by ~2022. *now a rule*
5. **The west side of the river** (x0-50): a second city half with its own waterfront; needs bridges. *untried*
6. **Cut running costs**: fire/police funding below 100%, transit funding; ordinances only where they pay. *untried*
7. **Taxes without caps**: very low early to speed growth, much higher on zones whose demand is capped. *partly tried*

# Attempt 1 strategy (written Jan 2000, before play)

Goal: 100,000 people by Jan 2050. Medium ($10,000), disasters on, loans allowed, flat map.

## What carried over from Claudeville I and II
- Check the city's health every January from the save file (water, power, abandoned, jammed roads,
  unemployment) and fix problems within the same year.
- Zones must touch a road. Power lines must touch side by side. Pumps go by the river.
- A link to a neighbor city brings outside trade (commercial and industrial demand).
- Low taxes help growth while the city is small.

## Plan
1. **Day one:** main avenue to the north map edge, a grid of east-west streets every 7 tiles
   (6-tile zone bands, 3 rows facing each street), coal plant, river pumps with pipes under every zone row.
   Homes nearest the river, shops in the middle, factories at the far end next to the coal plant.
2. **Grow:** zone what the demand bars ask for; keep taxes low until the city earns real money.
3. **Services early** because disasters are on: fire station, then police, as soon as income allows.
   Then schools and hospitals so dense buildings appear.
4. **Scale up:** extend the same grid east and south across the empty half of the map, then the west bank.
   Use rail or subway when traffic jams appear. Arcologies when they unlock.
5. **Loans:** allowed. Use a bond only for growth (more grid, power, transit) or a disaster rebuild, and keep
   yearly payments well below income.

## Checkpoints (pace needed for 100,000)
| Year | Population needed (straight line) |
|---|---|
| 2010 | 20,000 |
| 2020 | 40,000 |
| 2030 | 60,000 |
| 2040 | 80,000 |
| 2050 | 100,000 |

Growth in SimCity is slow at first and faster later, so being behind the straight line early is expected.
If the city is far behind by 2030 (well under 30,000) the attempt is very likely lost.
